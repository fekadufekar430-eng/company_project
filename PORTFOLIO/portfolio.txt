
    ***   The reason I suggested following this approach is that
          it helps make things much easier to understand.

When working on this kind of project, instead of having different people
write separate files—like one person writing the HTML, another writing the CSS,
and someone else writing the JavaScript (which can lead to misunderstandings or integration issues)
one person builds the entire page completely by themselves. This means they combine and write
everything (HTML, CSS, DOM manipulation, and JavaScript) all in one go, complete the full work,
and then push the finished, fully integrated code.
